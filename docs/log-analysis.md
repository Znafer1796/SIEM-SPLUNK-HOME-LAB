### Network Traffic Analysis Before and After Nmap Scanning

**Before the Nmap scan:** The baseline Splunk search `index="main"` returned 8,399 events, providing an overview of the network activity collected from OPNsense firewall logs.

**After the Nmap scan:** The search `index="main" filterlog "block"` returned 5,427 events containing the keyword `block`. OPNsense Live View also displayed multiple blocked TCP events. These results demonstrate that Splunk can search firewall logs and that OPNsense records blocked traffic.

**Assessment:** The evidence confirms centralized firewall log collection and visibility into blocked traffic. However, the two Splunk searches were captured with different or uncontrolled time ranges, so their event counts cannot be directly compared to measure the impact of Nmap. The available screenshots also do not conclusively establish that the observed blocked events were generated by the scan. Correlation of timestamps, source and destination IP addresses, destination ports, and firewall rules is required to verify this relationship.
